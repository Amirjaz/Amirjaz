## Amir Jazayeri

Computer Engineering student at Sharif University of Technology.
Software engineer at Nisatech.

Started out in frontend. These days I spend most of my time on the
backend — schema design, service architecture, and the parts of a
product users never see — and increasingly on AI engineering.

**Currently building** — [Letterboxd Toolkit](https://toolkit.nisatech.org),
a Chrome and Firefox extension that turns a Letterboxd diary into a full stats
profile, and puts your friends' ratings on every film page. Three tiers —
extension client, stateless API, cache server — over a ~30-table normalized
PostgreSQL replica of TMDB, with typed SQL through Kysely. Privacy is a design
constraint rather than a policy: the API only ever receives film slugs and
ratings, and persists nothing. No accounts, no tracking, nothing stored
server-side. 100+ installs across both stores.
